## Excerpt (paraphrase / key lines)

Karpathy-style second brain on Grok Bot:

- Three bots in one group, separate memory:
  - Reader: takes every link/PDF/voice note; never edits sources
  - Librarian: rewrites every wiki page a source touches (often 10–15)
  - Checker: nightly hunts contradictions, stale claims, orphan pages
- Ask a question → answer filed back as a page (brain grows from use)
- New source contradicts old page → Checker tags you; no silent overwrite
- Page 30 days without fresh source → marked unconfirmed
- Every page must show: claim, source, last confirmed date
- Folders: `raw/` untouched; `wiki/` maintained by librarian
- Start with **one topic** you actually work on, not whole life

Prompt sketch:

> Build me a second brain with three bots in one group chat. Keep my sources untouched in raw/, maintain wiki/ yourself, file good answers back as pages and run a nightly check for contradictions. Show me the structure first. Do not create anything until I confirm.
